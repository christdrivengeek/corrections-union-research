# Minnesota (MN) - Corrections Pay & Benefits Profile

**Official Agency:** Minnesota Department of Corrections (MNDOC)  
**Union Status:** `Unionized (Collective Bargaining)`  
**Union Representation:** Teamsters Local 320  
**Right-to-Work State:** No

## 1. Executive Summary
Minnesota Department of Corrections is unionized with Teamsters Local 320. Correctional Officers progress through a step system to reach the top salary, and they benefit from a defined benefit pension plan and state health insurance.

---

## 2. Base Compensation & Step Progression

| Pay Tier / Metric | Amount | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cadet / Academy Hourly** | `$15.76` | Entry rate during academy |
| **Starting Annual Base** | `$32,894` | Baseline entry annual pay |
| **Certified Officer Base** | `$40,000` | After certification/probation |
| **Top-Out Base Salary** | `$80,000` | Maximum regular base pay |
| **Years to Top Step** | `10 years` | Typical career progression |

**Step Progression System:**  
Correctional Officers progress through 10 steps with annual increases.

---

## 3. Retirement, Pension & Benefits

* **Retirement System:** Minnesota State Retirement System
* **Plan Type:** `Defined Benefit Pension`
* **Pension Multiplier Formula:** 2.5% at 57, 3% at 50, Rule of 80
* **Vesting Period:** 10 years
* **Employee Contribution:** 6%
* **Health Benefits:** Minnesota State Health Insurance Program (MSHIP) covers medical, dental, vision, and prescription drugs.
* **Hazardous Duty / Differentials:** $2.50 per hour for night duty, $3.75 per hour for weekend duty
