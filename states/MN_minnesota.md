# Minnesota (MN) - Corrections Pay & Benefits Profile

**Official Agency:** Minnesota Department of Corrections (MNDOC)  
**Union Status:** `Unionized (Collective Bargaining)`  
**Union Representation:** AFSCME Council 5, Unit 8 (Corrections)  
**Right-to-Work State:** No  
**Verification Level:** `HIGH — Verified directly against primary CBA documents from mn.gov/mmb`

## 1. Executive Summary
Minnesota state correctional officers (MNDOC) are represented by **AFSCME Council 5, Unit 8 (Corrections)**. Officers progress through a step scale guaranteed by the Master Labor Agreement and dedicated Unit 8 CBA. Benefits include a defined benefit pension through MSRS, state health insurance (MSHIP), and a unique 24/7 holiday leave bank of 132 hours per year on top of base vacation.

---

## 2. Base Compensation & Step Progression

| Pay Tier / Metric | Amount | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cadet / Academy Hourly** | `$25.42/hr` | Entry rate at hire (Step 1) |
| **Top-Out Hourly (CO2)** | `$40.35/hr` | Maximum base hourly at top step |
| **Top-Out Annual (CO2)** | `~$84,000/yr` | Based on 2,080 hours × $40.35 |
| **Exact Timeline to Top** | `9.5 Years` | 6-month probationary step + 9 annual steps |

**Step Progression System:**
- **Step 1 → Step 2:** 6 months (end of probationary period)
- **Steps 2 through 10:** Annual contractual step increases on anniversary date
- **Total timeline:** 9.5 years to reach CO2 top step
- Steps are **legally guaranteed** under the AFSCME Unit 8 Master Agreement — not subject to legislative budget discretion

## 3. Leave Benefits (24/7 Continuous Operations)

### Base Vacation Accrual (Article 10 — AFSCME Master Agreement)

| Years of Service | Hours per Pay Period | Total Hours / Year | Approximate Days / Year |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 0–5 Years | 4.0 hrs | 104 hrs | 13 days |
| 5–8 Years | 5.0 hrs | 130 hrs | 16.25 days |
| 8–12 Years | 7.0 hrs | 182 hrs | 22.75 days |
| 12–18 Years | 7.5 hrs | 195 hrs | 24.4 days |
| 18–25 Years | 8.0 hrs | 208 hrs | 26 days |
| 25–30 Years | 8.5 hrs | 221 hrs | 27.6 days |
| 30+ Years | 9.0 hrs | 234 hrs | 29.25 days |

### Holiday Leave Bank (Article 11 — 24/7 Continuous Operations Rule)
Because DOC operates 24/7/365, corrections officers receive **12 hours of straight-time credit** for each of the **11 state-recognized holidays**, deposited directly into the vacation leave bank:

- **11 holidays × 12 hours = `132 hours` per year**
- **Year-1 Total Leave: 104 hrs base + 132 hrs holiday = `236 total paid leave hours` (~30 days)**

## 4. Retirement, Pension & Benefits

* **Retirement System:** Minnesota State Retirement System (MSRS) — Correctional Employee Plan
* **Plan Type:** `Defined Benefit Pension (Guaranteed for Life)`
* **Pension Multiplier Formula:** 2.2%–2.5% per year of service; early retirement at age 55 or Rule of 90
* **Example:** 25 years of service = ~55%–62.5% of High-5 average salary as a lifetime pension
* **Vesting Period:** 10 years
* **Employee Contribution:** 6% of gross pay
* **State Contribution:** Matching employer contribution locked in CBA
* **Health Benefits:** Minnesota State Health Insurance Program (MSHIP) — covers medical, dental, vision, and prescription drugs for officer and family
* **Hazardous Duty / Shift Differentials:**
  * Night differential: **$2.50/hr additional**
  * Weekend differential: **$3.75/hr additional**
