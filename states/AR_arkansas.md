## 1. Executive Summary
The Arkansas Division of Correction offers competitive salaries and benefits, including a defined benefit pension through APERS. The agency is unionized but operates under a right-to-work state law.

## 3. Retirement, Pension & Benefits

* **Retirement System:** APERS (Arkansas Public Employees Retirement System)
* **Plan Type:** `Defined Benefit Pension`
* **Pension Multiplier Formula:** 2.5% at 57, 3% at 50, Rule of 80
* **Vesting Period:** 10 years
* **Employee Contribution:** None
* **Health Benefits:** Life insurance, sick leave (8 hours monthly), annual leave (8 – 15 hours monthly), eleven paid holidays including birthday.
* **Hazardous Duty / Differentials:** Night/weekend/hazard differentials
