# Indiana (IN) - Corrections Pay & Benefits Profile

**Official Agency:** Indiana Department of Correction (IDOC)  
**Union Status:** `Non-Union (No Collective Bargaining)`  
**Union Representation:** None  
**Right-to-Work State:** Yes  
**Verification Level:** `HIGH — Verified via IN SPD`  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Indiana state employees do not have collective bargaining rights. Compensation relies entirely on state appropriations, and raises are based on legislative action or performance rather than a contractual timeline.

---

**Step Progression System:**  
- The Indiana State Personnel Department (SPD) does not utilize a traditional, guaranteed step-progression system for Correctional Officers. 

---

## 3. Retirement, Pension & Benefits

* **Retirement System:** Indiana Public Retirement System (INPRS) - PERF
* **Plan Type:** `Hybrid`
* **Details:** Consists of a Defined Benefit (employer-funded traditional pension) and a Defined Contribution (employee-funded Annuity Savings Account).
