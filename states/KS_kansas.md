# Kansas (KS) - Corrections Pay & Benefits Profile

## 1. Executive Summary
The Kansas Department of Corrections is unionized with AFSCME Council 21. Employees receive a step-based salary system with a top step of $71,400 after 20 years of service and participate in the KPERS defined benefit pension plan.

**Step Progression System:**  
Employees advance through a step system based on years of service and performance. Each step represents an increase in salary.

## 3. Retirement, Pension & Benefits

* **Retirement System:** Kansas Public Employees Retirement System (KPERS)
* **Plan Type:** `Defined Benefit Pension`
* **Pension Multiplier Formula:** 2.5% at 57, 3% at 50, Rule of 80
* **Vesting Period:** 10 years
* **Employee Contribution:** 6%
* **Health Benefits:** Employees are covered under the Kansas State Health Insurance Program (KSHIP), which provides comprehensive health insurance coverage.
* **Hazardous Duty / Differentials:** $2.50 per hour for night duty, $3.00 per hour for weekend duty

## 5. Deep Dive: Overtime, Assault Pay, & Security

**Mandatory Overtime:** The KOSE union agreement states officers generally cannot be mandated to work more than 12 hours in a 24-hour period, except in emergencies (up to 18 hours).
**Assault / Line-of-Duty Injury Pay:** Standard Workers' Comp only (66.67%). No 100% "Assault Pay" statute exists.
**Job Security:** Protected by "Just Cause" under the Kansas Civil Service Act and KOSE union agreement.
**Seniority Bidding:** Bidding uses a weighted formula combining seniority (1 point per month of service), performance evaluations (8 points for Excellent), and experience on post.
