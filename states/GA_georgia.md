## 1. Executive Summary
The Georgia Department of Corrections employs correctional officers through a unionized structure, offering competitive salaries and benefits including a defined benefit pension plan.

## 3. Retirement, Pension & Benefits

* **Retirement System:** Georgia Retirement System
* **Plan Type:** `Defined Benefit Pension`
* **Pension Multiplier Formula:** 2.5% at 57, 3% at 50, Rule of 80
* **Vesting Period:** 10 years
* **Employee Contribution:** 6%
* **Health Benefits:** Employees receive comprehensive health insurance coverage through the Georgia Department of Corrections.
* **Hazardous Duty / Differentials:** Night and weekend duties are compensated with additional pay.
