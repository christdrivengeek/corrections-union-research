# Maryland (MD) - Corrections Pay & Benefits Profile

**Official Agency:** Maryland Department of Public Safety and Correctional Services (DPSCS)  
**Union Status:** `Unionized (Collective Bargaining)`  
**Union Representation:** AFSCME Maryland Council 3 (Unit H - Public Safety and Security)  
**Right-to-Work State:** No  
**Verification Level:** `HIGH — Verified via DBM Salary Schedules`  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Maryland state correctional officers are unionized under **AFSCME Council 3**. They have a long 25-step scale with a very high ceiling (up to ~$58.00/hr for upper CO grades), but step progression is strictly contingent on legislative budget funding rather than automatic longevity guarantees.

---

## 2. Base Compensation & Step Progression

| Pay Tier / Metric | Amount | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Top-Out Base Salary** | `$87,156 to $120,651` | Grades CO 0006 - CO 0011 |
| **Top-Out Hourly Rate** | `~$41.90 to ~$58.00` | Derived from annual base |
| **Exact Timeline to Top** | `25 Steps` | Step increases depend on state budget |

**Step Progression System:**
- The State of Maryland Correctional Salary Schedule utilizes 25 steps. 
- Step advancement is **not automatic**; it is strictly contingent upon funding appropriated in the annual State budget.

---

## 3. Retirement, Pension & Benefits

* **Retirement System:** Correctional Officers' Retirement System (CORS)
* **Plan Type:** `Defined Benefit Pension`
* **Retirement Eligibility:** 20 years of service (no age requirement).
