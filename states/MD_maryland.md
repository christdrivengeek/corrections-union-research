## 1. Executive Summary
The Maryland Department of Public Safety and Correctional Services is unionized, with AFSCME representing the bargaining unit. Officers receive a starting salary of $50,284 annually and can reach a top step salary of $137,696 annually through step increases contingent on state funding. The pension plan offers a defined benefit formula with vesting after 10 years.
