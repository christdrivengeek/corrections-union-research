## 3. Retirement, Pension & Benefits

* **Retirement System:** Utah State Retirement System
* **Plan Type:** `Defined Benefit Pension`
* **Pension Multiplier Formula:** 3% of final average salary for each year of service up to a maximum of 40 years.
* **Vesting Period:** 10 years
* **Employee Contribution:** No employee contribution is required.
* **Health Benefits:** Employees are eligible for state health insurance coverage, which includes medical, dental, and vision benefits.
