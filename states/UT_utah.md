# Utah (UT) - Corrections Pay & Benefits Profile

**Official Agency:** Utah Department of Corrections (UDC)  
**Union Status:** `Unionized (Collective Bargaining Restored)`  
**Union Representation:** UCPOA (FOP Lodge 14) / UPEA  
**Right-to-Work State:** Yes  
**Verification Level:** `HIGH`  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Utah recently restored public safety collective bargaining in 2025. Officers are represented by UCPOA (FOP Lodge 14).

---

## 2. Base Compensation & Step Progression

| Pay Tier / Metric | Amount | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Top-Out Hourly Rate** | `$40.24/hr` | Step 10 maximum hourly rate |
| **Exact Timeline to Top** | `10 Years` | 10-step plan |

**Step Progression System:**  
Employees advance through a 10-step certified pay plan. UDC also offers "1 for 1" years of service credit, allowing entry pay to be adjusted upward for prior corrections/law enforcement experience.

---

## 3. Retirement, Pension & Benefits

* **Retirement System:** Utah Retirement Systems (URS)
* **Plan Type:** `Hybrid or Defined Contribution` 

## 5. Deep Dive: Overtime, Assault Pay, & Security

**Mandatory Overtime:** No specific state laws or caps limiting hours. Overtime driven by operational needs. Once 480 hours of comp time is accrued, state must pay cash.
**Assault / Line-of-Duty Injury Pay:** 100% Pay. Utah Code § 63A-17-512 guarantees 100% of regular monthly salary and benefits if injured by a criminal act.
**Job Security:** "Just Cause" (career service status) after completing a one-year probationary period.
**Seniority Bidding:** No state law or CBA enforcing seniority-based bidding. Specific policies granting strict rights are not formalized.
