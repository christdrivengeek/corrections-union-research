# Arizona (AZ) - Corrections Pay & Benefits Profile

**Official Agency:** Arizona Department of Corrections, Rehabilitation & Reentry (ADCRR)  
**Union Status:** `Non-Union (Right-to-Work)`  
**Union Representation:** None (Associations only: AZCPOA / FOP Lodge 44)  
**Right-to-Work State:** Yes  
**Verification Level:** `HIGH — Verified via ADOA Salary Schedules`  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Arizona state correctional officers operate in a non-union environment with no collective bargaining rights. There is no traditional tenure-based step progression; pay increases rely entirely on legislative appropriations or promotions. 

---

## 2. Base Compensation & Step Progression

| Pay Tier / Metric | Amount | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Top-Out Hourly (CO)** | `~$26.43/hr` | Top Annual Salary: $54,975 |
| **Exact Timeline to Top** | `N/A` | No guaranteed step progression |

**Step Progression System:**
- Arizona **does not** utilize a traditional step ladder for ADCRR. Salary progression is highly subjective and depends on legislative budgets rather than guaranteed union-negotiated steps.
- Note: Some remote facilities (Winslow, Apache) offer 5-15% geographical stipends.

---

## 3. Retirement, Pension & Benefits

* **Retirement System:** Corrections Officer Retirement Plan (CORP) / PSPRS
* **Plan Type:** `Defined Contribution (401(a))` for officers hired after July 1, 2018. (Legacy officers have defined benefit).

## 5. Deep Dive: Overtime, Assault Pay, & Security

**Mandatory Overtime:** No limits. Frequent mandatory OT due to shortages.
**Assault / Line-of-Duty Injury Pay:** A recent state law (A.R.S. § 38-961) created a Supplemental Benefits Plan. If injured in an assault, it pays the difference so the officer receives approximately 100% of their base salary while workers' comp pays the rest.
**Job Security:** Recent legislative change (Sept 2024) transitioned them from at-will into the state's "covered service," giving them "just cause" protections, though not under a binding union arbitrator.
**Seniority Bidding:** Not a right. ADCRR internal policy uses it informally, but management retains ultimate discretion over shift bidding.
