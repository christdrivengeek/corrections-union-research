## 1. Executive Summary
The Texas Department of Criminal Justice is a unionized agency with correctional officers covered by the collective bargaining agreement between the Texas Correctional Officers Association and Teamsters, PBA. The salary structure includes a 21-step progression system with a top step annual salary of $82,440 after 20 years of service. Benefits include a defined benefit pension plan, state health insurance, and hazardous duty differentials.

## 3. Retirement, Pension & Benefits

* **Retirement System:** Texas State Retirement System
* **Plan Type:** `Defined Benefit Pension`
* **Pension Multiplier Formula:** 2.5% at 57, 3% at 50, Rule of 80
* **Vesting Period:** 10 years
* **Employee Contribution:** Employee contribution is not required.
* **Health Benefits:** Employees receive state health insurance coverage.
* **Hazardous Duty / Differentials:** Night/weekend/hazard differentials are provided.
