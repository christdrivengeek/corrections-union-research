# Maine (ME) - Corrections Pay & Benefits Profile

**Official Agency:** Maine Department of Corrections (MDOC)  
**Union Status:** `Unionized (Collective Bargaining)`  
**Union Representation:** AFSCME Council 93 (Institutional Services)  
**Right-to-Work State:** No  
**Verification Level:** `HIGH — Verified via BHR Pay Scales`  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Maine state correctional officers are represented by **AFSCME Council 93**. They have a fast 7-year step progression allowing them to reach a top rate of roughly $31.89/hr.

---

## 2. Base Compensation & Step Progression

| Pay Tier / Metric | Amount | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Top-Out Hourly Rate** | `~$31.89/hr` | Pay Grade 16 (includes stipend) |
| **Exact Timeline to Top** | `7 Years` | 8-step scale |

**Step Progression System:**  
- The Maine state pay scale has 8 steps. 
- Officers receive annual step increases for satisfactory performance, hitting the top step in 7 years.

---

## 3. Retirement, Pension & Benefits

* **Retirement System:** Maine Public Employees Retirement System (MainePERS)
* **Plan Type:** `Defined Benefit Pension (1998 Special Plan)`
* **Retirement Eligibility:** Age 55 with 10 years of service, or any age with 25 years of service.

## 5. Deep Dive: Overtime, Assault Pay, & Security

**Mandatory Overtime:** No statutory limits. Mandating practices are governed by the CBA, usually utilizing a mandation list.
**Assault / Line-of-Duty Injury Pay:** Standard Workers' Comp (80% of after-tax earnings). No automatic statutory guarantee of 100% pay unless specifically negotiated in the active contract language.
**Job Security:** Protected by "Just Cause". The CBA stipulates non-probationary employees cannot be discharged without cause and have arbitration rights.
**Seniority Bidding:** Yes. CBA outlines procedures for filling shift vacancies, posts, and transfers based heavily on seniority.
