## 1. Executive Summary
The Maine Department of Corrections is unionized under AFSCME Council 102, offering competitive salaries and benefits including a defined benefit pension system, comprehensive health insurance, and hazardous duty differentials. Employees progress through a step system to reach the top salary after 15 years.

## 3. Retirement, Pension & Benefits

* **Retirement System:** Maine Retirement System
* **Plan Type:** `Defined Benefit Pension`
* **Pension Multiplier Formula:** 2.5% at 57, 3% at 50, Rule of 80
* **Vesting Period:** 10 years
* **Employee Contribution:** No employee contribution required.
* **Health Benefits:** Employees are covered under the Maine Health Insurance Program (MHIP), which provides comprehensive health insurance coverage. Coverage includes medical, dental, vision, and prescription drugs.
* **Hazardous Duty / Differentials:** $1.50 per hour for night duty, $2.00 per hour for weekend duty.
