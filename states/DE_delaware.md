# Delaware (DE) - Corrections Pay & Benefits Profile

**Official Agency:** Delaware Department of Correction (DOC)  
**Union Status:** `Unionized (Collective Bargaining)`  
**Union Representation:** Correctional Officers Association of Delaware (COAD)  
**Right-to-Work State:** No  
**Verification Level:** `HIGH`  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Delaware officers are unionized under COAD and follow a Selective Market Variation (SMV) pay scale.

---

**Step Progression System:**  
The State of Delaware utilizes a Selective Market Variation (SMV) pay scale rather than a traditional step scale based strictly on years of service.

---

## 3. Retirement, Pension & Benefits

* **Retirement System:** State Employees' Pension Plan (Correctional)
* **Plan Type:** `Defined Benefit Pension` 

## 5. Deep Dive: Overtime, Assault Pay, & Security

**Mandatory Overtime:** No cap. Commonly referred to as "freezing", officers are routinely mandated for double shifts. Refusal is abandonment of post.
**Assault / Line-of-Duty Injury Pay:** 100% Pay Supplement. Under 29 Del. C. § 5933, the state supplements workers comp to make hazardous duty employees whole at 100% of regular salary for up to 12 months.
**Job Security:** Protected by "Just Cause" under the State Merit System and the union contract. Terminations can be appealed to the Merit Employee Relations Board (MERB).
**Seniority Bidding:** Yes. Negotiated by the COAD union, bidding for shifts and posts is traditionally awarded based on seniority.
