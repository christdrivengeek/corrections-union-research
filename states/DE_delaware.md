# Delaware (DE) - Corrections Pay & Benefits Profile

**Official Agency:** Delaware Department of Correction (DOC)  
**Union Status:** `Unionized (Collective Bargaining)`  
**Union Representation:** Correctional Officers Association of Delaware (COAD)  
**Right-to-Work State:** No  
**Verification Level:** `HIGH`  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Delaware officers are unionized under COAD and follow a Selective Market Variation (SMV) pay scale.

---

## 2. Base Compensation & Step Progression

| Pay Tier / Metric | Amount | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Top-Out Hourly Rate** | `~$36.78/hr` | Pay Grade C07 Maximum |
| **Exact Timeline to Top** | `N/A` | SMV open range |

**Step Progression System:**  
The State of Delaware utilizes a Selective Market Variation (SMV) pay scale rather than a traditional step scale based strictly on years of service.

---

## 3. Retirement, Pension & Benefits

* **Retirement System:** State Employees' Pension Plan (Correctional)
* **Plan Type:** `Defined Benefit Pension`
