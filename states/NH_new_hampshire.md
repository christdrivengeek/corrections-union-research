# New Hampshire (NH) - Corrections Pay & Benefits Profile

**Official Agency:** New Hampshire Department of Corrections  
**Union Status:** `Unionized (Collective Bargaining)`  
**Union Representation:** State Employees' Association of New Hampshire (SEA), SEIU Local 1984  
**Right-to-Work State:** No  
**Verification Level:** `HIGH`  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
New Hampshire state correctional officers are represented by SEIU Local 1984 and enjoy a dedicated police and fire pension (NHRS Group II) that does not participate in Social Security.

---

## 2. Base Compensation & Step Progression

| Pay Tier / Metric | Amount | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Top-Out Hourly Rate** | `~$35.23/hr` | Maximum step on current schedule |
| **Exact Timeline to Top** | `13 Years` | 9-step pay matrix |

**Step Progression System:**  
The NH state classified salary system uses a 9-step pay matrix. Historically, reaching Step 9 requires 13 years of service (progressing at intervals of 1, 1, 1, 1, 2, 2, 2, and 3 years).

---

## 3. Retirement, Pension & Benefits

* **Retirement System:** New Hampshire Retirement System (NHRS)
* **Plan Type:** `Defined Benefit Pension (Group II - Police and Fire)`
