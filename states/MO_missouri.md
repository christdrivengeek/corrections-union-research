## 1. Executive Summary
The Missouri Department of Corrections is unionized with the American Federation of State, County, and Municipal Employees (AFSCME) Council 72. Correctional Officers start at $45,000 annually and can reach a top step salary of $82,000 after 10 years. Benefits include a defined benefit pension system, major health insurance, life insurance, disability insurance, and cafeteria plans.
