# Wyoming (WY) - Corrections Pay & Benefits Profile

**Official Agency:** Wyoming Department of Corrections (WDOC)  
**Union Status:** `Non-Union (Right-to-Work)`  
**Union Representation:** None  
**Right-to-Work State:** Yes  
**Verification Level:** `HIGH — Verified via WY A&I`  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Wyoming state correctional officers are not represented by a union and do not have a guaranteed collective bargaining agreement. Wyoming utilizes broad pay bands rather than a predictable step plan, making career salary progression heavily reliant on merit and legislative budget approvals.

---

**Step Progression System:**
- Wyoming **does not** use a fixed step progression plan for this role.
- Pay increases are driven by merit, promotion, or legislative appropriations rather than automatic longevity steps.
