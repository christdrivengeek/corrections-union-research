## 1. Executive Summary
The Wyoming Department of Corrections is unionized with the AFSCME Council, Wyoming Division. Employees have access to a defined benefit pension plan, state health insurance, and various duty differentials. The salary structure includes steps for progression and differential pay for hazardous duties.

## 3. Retirement, Pension & Benefits

* **Retirement System:** Wyoming State Retirement System
* **Plan Type:** `Defined Benefit Pension`
* **Pension Multiplier Formula:** 3% at age 50, 4% at age 60, Rule of 70
* **Vesting Period:** 10 years
* **Employee Contribution:** No employee contribution required.
* **Health Benefits:** Employees are eligible for state health insurance coverage after completing probation. Coverage includes medical, dental, and vision benefits.
* **Hazardous Duty / Differentials:** Night duty differential is $2.00 per hour; weekend differential is $3.00 per hour.
