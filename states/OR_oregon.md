# Oregon (OR) - Corrections Pay & Benefits Profile

**Official Agency:** Oregon Department of Corrections (ODOC)  
**Union Status:** `Unionized (Collective Bargaining)`  
**Union Representation:** AFSCME Council 75  
**Right-to-Work State:** No  
**Verification Level:** `HIGH`  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Oregon correctional officers are represented by AFSCME Council 75 and benefit from a high top-out rate and the robust PERS Police & Fire pension system.

---

## 2. Base Compensation & Step Progression

| Pay Tier / Metric | Amount | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Top-Out Hourly Rate** | `~$46.04/hr` | Step 9/10 maximum base |
| **Exact Timeline to Top** | `8 - 9 Years` | Multi-step schedule |

**Step Progression System:**  
The state compensation plan uses a multi-step schedule (typically 9-10 steps), with a step advancement at 6 months and annually thereafter.

---

## 3. Retirement, Pension & Benefits

* **Retirement System:** Oregon PERS (Police & Fire)
* **Plan Type:** `Defined Benefit + Individual Account`
