# Hawaii (HI) - Corrections Pay & Benefits Profile

**Official Agency:** Hawaii Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation (DCR)  
**Union Status:** `Unionized (Collective Bargaining)`  
**Union Representation:** United Public Workers (UPW), AFSCME, Local 646, AFL-CIO (Bargaining Unit 10)  
**Right-to-Work State:** No  
**Verification Level:** `HIGH — Verified via Hawaii DHRD`  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Hawaii adult corrections officers are represented by the **United Public Workers (UPW) Unit 10**. They start at $33.18/hr during the academy and receive a defined benefit pension through the Hawaii ERS.

---

## 2. Base Compensation & Step Progression

| Pay Tier / Metric | Amount | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cadet / Academy Hourly** | `$33.18/hr` | Entry rate for Recruit (CO-04) |
| **Certified Officer Base** | `$34.62/hr` | Adult Corrections Officer III (CO-06) |
| **Top-Out Hourly Rate** | `Varies` | Subject to UPW longevity steps |

**Step Progression System:**  
The salary schedule includes multiple classes (e.g., CO-04, CO-06). Progression through longevity steps relies on the active collective bargaining agreement negotiated by UPW Unit 10 rather than purely automatic timelines.

---

## 3. Retirement, Pension & Benefits

* **Retirement System:** Hawaii Employees' Retirement System (ERS)
* **Plan Type:** `Defined Benefit Pension`
