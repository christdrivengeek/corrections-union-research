## 1. Executive Summary
The Hawaii Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation is unionized with Local 152, AFSCME, AFL-CIO representing various employee units. Certified officers start at $50,427 annually after probation, while the top step for rank-and-file positions is $138,690 annually. Employees receive comprehensive health insurance and a defined benefit pension system.

## 3. Retirement, Pension & Benefits

* **Retirement System:** State Retirement System
* **Plan Type:** `Defined Benefit Pension`
* **Pension Multiplier Formula:** 2% of final average salary at age 57, 3% at age 50.
* **Vesting Period:** 10 years of service to vest in full.
* **Employee Contribution:** Employee contributes 6.5% of salary up to a cap.
* **Health Benefits:** Employees receive comprehensive health insurance coverage including medical, dental, and vision benefits.
* **Hazardous Duty / Differentials:** Night duty differential is $10 per hour; weekend differential is $20 per hour.
