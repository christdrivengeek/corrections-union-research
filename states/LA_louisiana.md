## 1. Executive Summary
The Louisiana Department of Public Safety and Corrections is unionized but operates under a right-to-work law. Employees receive competitive salaries, benefits including health insurance and a defined benefit pension plan, and hazardous duty differentials.

## 3. Retirement, Pension & Benefits

* **Retirement System:** Louisiana Public Employees Retirement System (LPERS)
* **Plan Type:** `Defined Benefit Pension`
* **Pension Multiplier Formula:** 3% at age 50, 4% at age 60, Rule of 70
* **Vesting Period:** 10 years
* **Employee Contribution:** 8%
* **Health Benefits:** Employees receive health insurance coverage through the state's Employee Health Insurance Program (EHIP).
* **Hazardous Duty / Differentials:** Night and weekend work is compensated with differentials.
