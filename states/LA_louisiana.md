# Louisiana (LA) - Corrections Pay & Benefits Profile

**Official Agency:** Louisiana Department of Public Safety and Corrections  
**Union Status:** `Non-Union`  
**Union Representation:** None  
**Right-to-Work State:** Yes  
**Verification Level:** `HIGH`  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Louisiana is a Right-to-Work state with no formal collective bargaining rights for corrections officers.

---

---

## 3. Retirement, Pension & Benefits

* **Retirement System:** LASERS (Hazardous Duty Services Plan)
* **Plan Type:** `Defined Benefit Pension` 

## 5. Deep Dive: Overtime, Assault Pay, & Security

**Mandatory Overtime:** No limits. State Civil Service Rule 21.1 gives employers the authority to require overtime. Refusing is insubordination.
**Assault / Line-of-Duty Injury Pay:** Standard workers comp pays 66 2/3%. However, Civil Service Rule 11.21.1 allows the appointing authority to grant up to six months of leave with pay for law enforcement disabled in the course of employment.
**Job Security:** Permanent classified officers have "Just Cause" protections and due process rights to appeal to the State Civil Service Commission. Probationary employees are at-will.
**Seniority Bidding:** None. Management manages shift scheduling and bidding rules at the individual facility level based on operational needs.
