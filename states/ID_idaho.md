# Idaho (ID) - Corrections Pay & Benefits Profile

**Official Agency:** Idaho Department of Correction (IDOC)  
**Union Status:** `Non-Union`  
**Union Representation:** None  
**Right-to-Work State:** Yes  
**Verification Level:** `HIGH`  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Idaho is a right-to-work state without collective bargaining for state employees. 

---

## 2. Base Compensation & Step Progression

| Pay Tier / Metric | Amount | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Top-Out Hourly Rate** | `~$34.62/hr` | Maximum for standard CO |
| **Exact Timeline to Top** | `N/A` | No automatic steps |

**Step Progression System:**  
Idaho does not use automatic time-in-grade step progressions. Salary increases are determined by the annual Change in Employee Compensation (CEC) legislative process.

---

## 3. Retirement, Pension & Benefits

* **Retirement System:** PERSI (Public Employee Retirement System of Idaho)
* **Plan Type:** `Defined Benefit Pension`
