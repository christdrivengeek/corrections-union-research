# Idaho (ID) - Corrections Pay & Benefits Profile

## 1. Executive Summary
The Idaho Department of Correction employs correctional officers who are unionized under the National Correctional Employees Union. Officers receive a step-based salary starting at $42,900 and participate in a defined benefit pension plan with a ME Public Employees Retirement System. Benefits include health insurance and hazardous duty differentials.

**Step Progression System:**  
Correctional officers progress through a step system based on performance and experience. Each step represents an increase in pay.

---

## 3. Retirement, Pension & Benefits

* **Retirement System:** ME Public Employees Retirement System
* **Plan Type:** `Defined Benefit Pension`
* **Pension Multiplier Formula:** 3% of final average monthly earnings for each year of credited service up to a maximum of 40 years.
* **Vesting Period:** 20 years
* **Employee Contribution:** No employee contribution required.
* **Health Benefits:** Employees receive health insurance coverage through the state's Employee Health Insurance Program (EHIP). Coverage includes medical, dental, and vision benefits.
* **Hazardous Duty / Differentials:** Correctional officers receive differentials for night work, weekend work, and hazardous duty assignments.
