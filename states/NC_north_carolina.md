## 1. Executive Summary
North Carolina's Department of Adult Correction employs correctional officers with a competitive salary and step pay system, offering health insurance, pension benefits, and hazardous duty differentials. The National Correctional Employees Union represents the workforce in collective bargaining.

**Step Progression System:**  
Correctional officers progress through a step pay plan with annual increases based on performance and seniority.

---

## 3. Retirement, Pension & Benefits

* **Retirement System:** State pension retirement
* **Plan Type:** `Defined Benefit Pension`
* **Pension Multiplier Formula:** 3% at 50, 4% at 55, 5% at 60, 6% at 65
* **Vesting Period:** 10 years
* **Employee Contribution:** None
* **Health Benefits:** Health insurance starting at $50 a month with options for health, vision, and dental coverage.
* **Hazardous Duty / Differentials:** Night/weekend/hazard differentials provided.
