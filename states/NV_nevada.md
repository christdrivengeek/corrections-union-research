## 1. Executive Summary
The Nevada Department of Corrections is unionized, but does not have a collective bargaining agreement. Employees receive a step-based salary system with a top step of $76,800 and participate in the Nevada State Retirement System with a defined benefit formula.

## 3. Retirement, Pension & Benefits

* **Retirement System:** Nevada State Retirement System
* **Plan Type:** `Defined Benefit Pension`
* **Pension Multiplier Formula:** 2% of final average earnings per year of service up to a maximum of 30 years.
* **Vesting Period:** 10 years
* **Employee Contribution:** None
* **Health Benefits:** Employees are eligible for state health insurance coverage after one year of employment. Coverage includes medical, dental, and vision benefits.
* **Hazardous Duty / Differentials:** Night duty: $2.50 per hour; Weekend duty: $1.75 per hour.

## 5. Deep Dive: Overtime, Assault Pay, & Security

**Mandatory Overtime:** No statutory limits. Due to high vacancy rates, heavy reliance on mandatory overtime is legal. Refusing is insubordination.
**Assault / Line-of-Duty Injury Pay:** Standard Workers' Comp only (66.67%). No statutory 100% assault pay provision for state COs.
**Job Security:** Protected by "Just Cause". State COs are public, classified employees and secure just cause protections after completing probation.
**Seniority Bidding:** Yes. Driven heavily by seniority via the CBA, usually through an annual bid for preferred shifts and posts.
