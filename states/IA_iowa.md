# Iowa (IA) - Corrections Pay & Benefits Profile

**Official Agency:** Iowa Department of Corrections (IDOC)  
**Union Status:** `Unionized (Collective Bargaining - Limited)`  
**Union Representation:** AFSCME Council 61 (Public Safety Unit)  
**Right-to-Work State:** Yes  
**Verification Level:** `HIGH — Verified via DAS Pay Data`  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Iowa state correctional officers are represented by **AFSCME Council 61**. However, due to recent changes in Iowa collective bargaining laws, pay ranges are defined by minimums and maximums rather than automatic time-in-service steps.

---

## 2. Base Compensation & Step Progression

| Pay Tier / Metric | Amount | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Top-Out Hourly Rate** | `$33.33/hr` | FY2026 maximum for Pay Grade 23 |
| **Exact Timeline to Top** | `Not Fixed` | Tied to merit/ATB increases |

**Step Progression System:**  
- Not guaranteed/fixed. 
- Progression through the range is typically tied to merit-based policies or negotiated across-the-board (ATB) increases rather than automatic longevity steps.

---

## 3. Retirement, Pension & Benefits

* **Retirement System:** IPERS (Iowa Public Employees' Retirement System)
* **Plan Type:** `Defined Benefit Pension (Protection Occupation)`
