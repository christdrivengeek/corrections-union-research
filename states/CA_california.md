## 1. Executive Summary
The California Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation (CDCR) is unionized with the California Correctional Peace Officers Association (CCPOA). Correctional officers receive an annual salary up to $113,000 with available incentives, and benefits include a defined benefit pension plan and state health insurance. The CDCR offers exceptional benefits for correctional officers.
