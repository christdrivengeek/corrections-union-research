# Montana (MT) - Corrections Pay & Benefits Profile

**Official Agency:** Montana Department of Corrections  
**Union Status:** `Unionized (Collective Bargaining)`  
**Union Representation:** MFPE (COR Consortium 005)  
**Right-to-Work State:** No  
**Verification Level:** `HIGH`  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Montana officers are represented by MFPE and utilize a Broadband Pay Plan.

---

## 2. Base Compensation & Step Progression

| Pay Tier / Metric | Amount | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Top-Out Hourly Rate** | `~$29.36/hr` | Broadband Maximum |
| **Exact Timeline to Top** | `N/A` | No fixed steps |

**Step Progression System:**  
The state's Broadband Pay Plan does not use fixed annual step progressions. Progression is based on established entry, midpoint, and maximum hourly rates within pay bands.

---

## 3. Retirement, Pension & Benefits

* **Retirement System:** Game Wardens' and Peace Officers' Retirement System (GWPORS)
* **Plan Type:** `Defined Benefit Pension`
