Note: New part-time Correctional Officers attend the training academy for 8 weeks and on-the-job training at their unit of assignment for 1 week on a full-time basis. During this period, officers receive a full-time Correctional Officer I salary. For any remaining part of the second month of employment, the Correctional Officer receives the part-time salary of a Correctional Officer I.
