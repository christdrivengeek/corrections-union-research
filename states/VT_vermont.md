## 1. Executive Summary
Vermont's Department of Corrections is unionized under the CCPOA. Employees receive competitive salaries with a step system and a defined benefit pension plan. Health insurance coverage is comprehensive, and there are differentials for hazardous duties such as night and weekend work.

## 3. Retirement, Pension & Benefits

* **Retirement System:** Vermont Retirement System
* **Plan Type:** `Defined Benefit Pension`
* **Pension Multiplier Formula:** 3% at age 50, 4% at age 60, Rule of 70
* **Vesting Period:** 10 years
* **Employee Contribution:** No employee contribution required.
* **Health Benefits:** Employees receive comprehensive health insurance coverage through the Vermont Department of Corrections' health plan.
* **Hazardous Duty / Differentials:** $2.50 per hour for night duty, $3.00 per hour for weekend duty.
