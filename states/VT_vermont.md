# Vermont (VT) - Corrections Pay & Benefits Profile

**Official Agency:** Vermont Department of Corrections  
**Union Status:** `Unionized (Collective Bargaining)`  
**Union Representation:** Vermont State Employees' Association (VSEA) - Corrections Unit  
**Right-to-Work State:** No  
**Verification Level:** `HIGH`  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Vermont state correctional officers are represented by VSEA and follow a long 15-step scale that takes 22.5 years to reach the maximum rate.

---

## 2. Base Compensation & Step Progression

| Pay Tier / Metric | Amount | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Top-Out Hourly Rate** | `$36.51/hr` | Pay Grade 19, Step 15 |
| **Exact Timeline to Top** | `22.5 Years` | 15-step scale |

**Step Progression System:**  
Standard progression: Step 2 at 6 months; Steps 3-6 require 1 year in each step; Steps 7-15 require 2 years in each step.

---

## 3. Retirement, Pension & Benefits

* **Retirement System:** Vermont State Employees' Retirement System (VSERS)
* **Plan Type:** `Defined Benefit Pension (Group G)`
