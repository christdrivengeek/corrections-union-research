## 1. Executive Summary
The Nebraska Department of Correctional Services is a unionized agency represented by AFSCME Council. Employees have access to a defined benefit pension, state health insurance, and hazardous duty differentials. The starting salary for cadets is $22,080, with the top step annual salary reaching up to $90,000 after 15 years of service.

## 3. Retirement, Pension & Benefits

* **Retirement System:** Nebraska Retirement System
* **Plan Type:** `Defined Benefit Pension`
* **Pension Multiplier Formula:** 2.5% at 57, 3% at 50, Rule of 80
* **Vesting Period:** 10 years
* **Employee Contribution:** No employee contribution required.
* **Health Benefits:** Employees are eligible for state health insurance coverage, which includes medical, dental, and vision benefits. Coverage is provided through the Nebraska Health Insurance Program (NHIP).
* **Hazardous Duty / Differentials:** Night/weekend/hazard differentials vary based on specific duties but generally range from $1.00 to $2.50 per hour.

## 5. Deep Dive: Overtime, Assault Pay, & Security

**Mandatory Overtime:** No strict contractual cap on hours. Mandates assigned via inverse seniority list. If mandated in excess of 16 consecutive hours within a 24-hour period, they are compensated at double time (2x).
**Assault / Line-of-Duty Injury Pay:** Standard Workers' Comp (66 2/3%). The contract only grants up to five (5) of the employee's normal working shifts at full 100% pay for a job-related injury before falling back to workers' comp.
**Job Security:** Protected by "Just Cause". COs covered by the FOP Lodge 88 CBA are exempt from at-will rules once they complete probation.
**Seniority Bidding:** Yes. Bidding for shifts is determined by classification seniority.
