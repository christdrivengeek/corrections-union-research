# Michigan (MI) - Corrections Pay & Benefits Profile

**Official Agency:** Michigan Department of Corrections (MDOC)  
**Union Status:** `Unionized (Collective Bargaining)`  
**Union Representation:** Michigan Corrections Organization (MCO - SEIU Local 526M)  
**Right-to-Work State:** Repealed (Union security agreements permitted)  
**Verification Level:** `HIGH — Verified against MCSC Pay Schedules`  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Michigan state correctional officers are represented by the **Michigan Corrections Organization (MCO)**. Officers progress through a rapid step scale, reaching the top rate in just 4.5 years.

---

## 2. Base Compensation & Step Progression

| Pay Tier / Metric | Amount | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Top-Out Hourly (CO E9)** | `$35.23/hr` | Maximum base hourly at top step |
| **Exact Timeline to Top** | `4.5 Years (54 mos)`| Typical career progression to top step |

**Step Progression System:**
- Step increases are guaranteed by the MCO collective bargaining agreement and MCSC pay plan.

---

## 3. Retirement, Pension & Benefits

* **Retirement System:** State Employees Retirement System
* **Plan Type:** `Defined Contribution (401(k)-style)` for officers hired post-1997. Legacy officers retain Defined Benefit.
* **Health Benefits:** Comprehensive state health, dental, and vision insurance.
