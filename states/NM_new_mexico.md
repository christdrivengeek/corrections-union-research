# New Mexico (NM) - Corrections Pay & Benefits Profile

**Official Agency:** New Mexico Corrections Department (NMCD)  
**Union Status:** `Unionized (Collective Bargaining)`  
**Union Representation:** AFSCME Council 18  
**Right-to-Work State:** No  
**Verification Level:** `HIGH — Verified via SPO Pay Bands`  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
New Mexico state correctional officers are represented by **AFSCME Council 18**. They operate under a pay band structure rather than fixed steps.

---

## 2. Base Compensation & Step Progression

| Pay Tier / Metric | Amount | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Top-Out Hourly Rate** | `$33.93 - $37.32/hr` | Pay Band C5 - C6 Maximum |
| **Exact Timeline to Top** | `N/A` | Broadbanding / Pay Bands |

**Step Progression System:**  
- New Mexico uses a classified Pay Band structure with minimum, midpoint, and maximum rates rather than a traditional year-by-year step progression.
- Advancement relies on merit increases and legislative budget allocations.

---

## 3. Retirement, Pension & Benefits

* **Retirement System:** New Mexico Public Employees Retirement Association (PERA)
* **Plan Type:** `Defined Benefit Pension`
* **Public Safety Nuance:** Provides a 20% service credit enhancement for adult correctional officers.
