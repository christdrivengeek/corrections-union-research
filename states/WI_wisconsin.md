# Wisconsin (WI) - Corrections Pay & Benefits Profile

**Official Agency:** Wisconsin Department of Corrections (WDOC)  
**Union Status:** `Unionized (Limited by Act 10)`  
**Union Representation:** Limited  
**Right-to-Work State:** Yes  
**Verification Level:** `HIGH`  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Wisconsin officers are subject to Act 10, strictly limiting collective bargaining to base wage increases capped at inflation (CPI). 

---

## 2. Base Compensation & Step Progression

| Pay Tier / Metric | Amount | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Top-Out Hourly Rate** | `$39.86/hr` | Maximum base |
| **Exact Timeline to Top** | `20 Years` | Progression milestones |

**Step Progression System:**  
Progression milestones are traditionally set at 1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 15, and 20 years of continuous service.

---

## 3. Retirement, Pension & Benefits

* **Retirement System:** Wisconsin Retirement System (WRS)
* **Plan Type:** `Defined Benefit (Protective Occupation Status)`
