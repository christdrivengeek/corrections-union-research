# Rhode Island (RI) - Corrections Pay & Benefits Profile

**Official Agency:** Rhode Island Department of Corrections (RIDOC)  
**Union Status:** `Unionized (Collective Bargaining)`  
**Union Representation:** Rhode Island Brotherhood of Correctional Officers (RIBCO)  
**Right-to-Work State:** No  
**Verification Level:** `HIGH — Verified via RI Human Resources`  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Rhode Island state correctional officers are strongly unionized under the **Rhode Island Brotherhood of Correctional Officers (RIBCO)**. They feature one of the highest top-out rates on the east coast at nearly $50/hour, reached through a 15-year step progression.

---

## 2. Base Compensation & Step Progression

| Pay Tier / Metric | Amount | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Hourly** | `~$33.50/hr` | Entry rate ($69,687 annually) |
| **Top-Out Hourly** | `~$49.46/hr` | Maximum base ($102,878 annually) |
| **Exact Timeline to Top** | `15 Years` | Guaranteed 15-step schedule |

**Step Progression System:**  
- The salary progression is based on a structured 15-step salary schedule negotiated by RIBCO.
- Allows for predictable progression based strictly on years of service.

---

## 3. Retirement, Pension & Benefits

* **Retirement System:** Employees' Retirement System of Rhode Island (ERSRI)
* **Plan Type:** `Defined Benefit Pension (Fixed-Benefit)`
* **Retirement Eligibility:** Eligible at age 55 with 25 years of service.
