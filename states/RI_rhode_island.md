## 1. Executive Summary
The Rhode Island Department of Corrections is unionized with the Rhode Island Brotherhood of Correctional Officers. Correctional officers receive a starting salary of $55,000 per year and are eligible for a one-time bonus of $3,000 if employed from July 1, 2020, to the date of ratification.
